TITLE
The Hollow Reliquary - Gothic Metroidvania Environment Pack

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A modular 32x32 dark-fantasy sanctuary tileset with 47-tile autotiling, props,
animations, parallax layers and Tiled files.

SUGGESTED PRICE
US$5.00 regular price. Optional launch discount: 20% for 7 days.

DESCRIPTION
Build a forgotten sanctuary beneath a dying kingdom.

The Hollow Reliquary is a focused environment kit for side-scrolling action
games and metroidvanias. Its cold stone, decayed gold, wine-red cloth, hollow
saint sigils and restrained magical accents share one consistent scale, light
direction and palette.

WHAT YOU GET
- 47-piece 8-neighbor terrain autotile, plus moss variant
- 64 modular architecture tiles
- 23 separate props
- 6 animated environment elements with frames and spritesheets
- 3 parallax-ready 512x288 background layers
- Tiled TSX and sample TMX map
- PNG RGBA, manifest, palette, collision guide and integration notes

Designed for Unity, Godot, Unreal and custom engines. Use nearest-neighbor
filtering at integer scale for the intended look.
